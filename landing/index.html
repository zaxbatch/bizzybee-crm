FAQ

Good questions

Is BizzyBee CRM really free?

Yes — the Free plan is free forever, no credit card required. You get 1,000 contacts, 5 pipelines, deals, activities, the dashboard and CSV export at no cost. Paid plans (Pro $19/mo, Business $49/mo) unlock more capacity and power when you need it.

What's included in Pro and Business?

Pro adds unlimited contacts, 20 pipelines, 50 custom fields, API access, basic workflow automation and custom reports. Business adds unlimited pipelines and custom fields, full API access, white-label rebranding with your own domain, priority support (1–4hr response), advanced automation, and AI-powered insights. See the pricing table.

Who is behind BizzyBee CRM?

BizzyBee CRM is brought to you by Z Dot LLC.

Can I export my data?

Always. Every view has a one-click CSV export, so your data is never locked in.

What happens to my sign-up info?

Your name and email go straight into our CRM so we can follow up with you personally. We never sell your data.
